Good questions

Frequently asked

Do I use my own Facebook ad account?

Yes. ClickNest never runs ads on some shared account — you connect your own ad account and Pages, and every campaign runs there. You keep ownership and billing stays with you.

How does ClickNest connect to Facebook?

Through Meta's official Marketing API using Facebook Login. You authorize the specific permissions you're comfortable with, and you can revoke them at any time from your Facebook settings.

What can I actually do inside ClickNest?

Create, launch, edit and pause campaigns; manage budgets in bulk; and track spend, clicks, conversions and ROAS per campaign and per ad in real time.

Is my data sold or shared?

No. We access your data only to operate the features you use, and we don't sell it or share it with third parties beyond what's required to provide the service (such as Meta's own APIs). See our Privacy Policy.

Can my team use it together?

Yes — ClickNest supports owners, admins and media buyers, with each company's campaigns and accounts kept cleanly separated.

How do I get started?

Create a free account, connect your Facebook ad account, and launch your first campaign. It takes a few minutes.
